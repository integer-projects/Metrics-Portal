Precision Liner Metrics Portal - Associate SOP

Version 3.0 - effective August 13, 2026

This page replaces the retired multi-user and hour-by-hour PL instructions. The current production workflow uses the isolated /pl/ page, a server-owned workspace, PostgreSQL capture, and background Smartsheet synchronization.

Sign in and verify identity

  1. Select Precision Liner on the login page.
  2. Select your own name, enter your password, and log in.
  3. Confirm the Precision Liner heading and your name before entering data.

Job and event entry

  • Jobs require sequence, lot, a six-digit item, positive time, quantities, defects, and any required quality details.
  • Notes are required below 75% yield. At 50% or lower, complete at least one expanded root-cause field.
  • Spool Check requires its sequence and check number; Reason for Fail is separate from ordinary Notes.
  • Events require the event name and total duration in minutes.
  • Choose Save to database once and wait for the form to clear.

Status rules

  • Saved to server protects the form workspace.
  • Database saved - Smartsheet pending means the entry is safe. Continue working; no repeated refresh is required.
  • Database saved - Smartsheet synced means the destination row is confirmed.
  • Do not re-enter database-saved work to force synchronization.
  • For a tab conflict, stop editing and choose Load server copy.

Sign out and support

Sign out only after unfinished work is saved or intentionally discarded. Report database-save failures, failed/needs-review status, pending over five minutes, stale login locks, or incorrect calculations to a supervisor. Include your name, workstation, time, entry type, exact message, and a screenshot when possible. Never share your password.
