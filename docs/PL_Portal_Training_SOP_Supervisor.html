Precision Liner Metrics Portal - Supervisor and Support SOP

Version 2.0 - effective August 13, 2026

The Metrics Portal database is the operational record. Smartsheet is the downstream destination. Never ask an associate to re-enter a database-saved submission merely because synchronization is pending.

Submission monitoring

  • Open Admin, then Submission Status.
  • pending and processing are active delivery work; five minutes is the alert threshold.
  • submitted confirms the destination row.
  • failed and needs_review require immediate review.
  • Retry only after correcting or confirming the cause; Resolve only after approved reconciliation. Both require a specific audited reason.

Associate support

Collect the associate, workstation, work date/time, entry type, exact banner/error, and screenshot. Saved-to-server describes the form; database-saved confirms the production record. Load the server copy after a tab conflict. Release a stale workstation lock only when the original session cannot be ended and the lock is not active.

Daily controls

  1. Confirm metrics-portal and metrics-portal-worker are online.
  2. Confirm liveness, readiness, PostgreSQL, queue, backup result/freshness, and disk are healthy.
  3. Investigate terminal items immediately and active delivery older than five minutes.

Safety boundaries

  • Do not delete production rows to make counts match.
  • Do not edit PostgreSQL submission/outbox rows manually.
  • Do not expose passwords, tokens, or environment files.
  • Do not restart the stopped legacy PL portal except under approved rollback.
